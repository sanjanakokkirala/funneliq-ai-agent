## How it works (algorithm)

1\) Integrate ad platforms, web analytics, CRM and billing with identity resolution → 2) Build each lead’s journey: touchpoints → lead → MQL → SQL → opportunity → won → revenue → 3) Measure stage conversion and compare with benchmarks → 4) Attribute revenue with first, last, linear, time-decay and Markov removal-effect models → 5) Score lead quality by channel (revenue per lead, cost per revenue) → 6) Quantify value lost at each stage = (benchmark − actual rate) × entrants × downstream yield → 7) Agent recommends fixes.
